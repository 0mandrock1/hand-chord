Hand Chord

A webcam and a pair of hands is the whole instrument. Your left hand chooses the chord, your right hand shapes it.

Video never leaves your device — hand tracking runs locally in the browser.

Guide

Left hand — which chord

Tilt

  • lean inward → major
  • lean outward → minor

Fingers — scale degree

  • 1 finger → I
  • 2 → II
  • 3 → III
  • 4 → IV
  • 5 → V
  • index + pinky → VI
  • index + pinky + thumb → VII

Closed fist = silence.

Right hand — how it sounds

Fingers — chord quality

  • 1 finger → root position
  • 2 → 1st inversion
  • 3 → major / minor 7th
  • 4 → dominant / diminished 7th

Thumb — octave

  • tucked in → higher octave
  • stuck out → lower octave

Tilt — filter

  • lean inward → brighter
  • lean outward → darker

Height — volume

  • raise the hand → louder
  • lower it → softer

If the hands read backwards, tick Swap hands — camera drivers disagree about mirroring.
